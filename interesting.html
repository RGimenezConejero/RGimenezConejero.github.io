Physics

Sonic black holes

If a fluid flows faster than sound, sound waves can become trapped behind a boundary that behaves a little like the event horizon of a black hole. It is a clever way to explore ideas such as Hawking radiation in a laboratory, without needing an actual black hole.

Overview
  • Unruh, “Experimental Black-Hole Evaporation?” (1981)

Physics

The Unruh effect

What does empty space look like when you are accelerating? According to quantum field theory, an accelerating observer would detect a warm bath of particles where an observer moving at constant speed sees only vacuum.

Overview
  • Fulling, “Nonuniqueness of Canonical Field Quantization in Riemannian Space-Time” (1973)
  • Davies, “Scalar Production in Schwarzschild and Rindler Metrics” (1975)
  • Unruh, “Notes on Black-Hole Evaporation” (1976)

Language and cognition

Arabic morphology and the mental lexicon

Many Arabic words are built by fitting consonantal roots into different patterns. This raises a question: when we learn a word, what exactly does the mind store? The whole word, its root, its pattern, or something smaller? The papers below approach the question through experiments and through unusual language errors found in aphasia and deep dyslexia.

A YouTube short
  • Prunet, Béland & Idrissi, “The Mental Representation of Semitic Words” (2000)
  • Boudelaa & Marslen-Wilson, “Structure, Form, and Meaning in the Mental Lexicon: Evidence from Arabic” (2015)
  • Idrissi & Kehayia, “Morphological Units in the Arabic Mental Lexicon: Evidence from an Individual with Deep Dyslexia” (2004)
